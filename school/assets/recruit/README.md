# Pixel recruitment illustrations

These three illustrations were generated from the user's approved character designs and revised poses for the recruitment screen. Source generation PNGs remain in the workspace; the shipped WebP files retain the full 1672×941 resolution (quality 92), without visual rescaling or retouching. The three encoded files total about 1 MB.

- `ryusion.webp`: female earth defender, understated face, giant slate shield and golden earth effects.
- `osia.webp`: water Dragon King, raised-arm surfing pose and serpentine water dragon; gold fins, white stockings and brown boots preserved.
- `lava.webp`: male wind healer, firmer jaw/brows/shoulders, forward fan sweep; Korean shaman outer skirt and trousers preserved.

The banners intentionally contain no baked-in UI text. Names, rarity, price, availability and pity remain accessible live game controls. Recruitment uses these assets only; battle sprites, skill animation atlases and gameplay rules are separate.
